MSSG project

The MSSG-project was a school project where our group had to create a prototype and present it to the public. This project is one of the reasons why i want to start my own business. The software we used to code in is called Arduino IDE. We used the Freenove ultimate starter kit to create this prototype. The picture on the right is my group presenting our prototype. Details on the prototype: MSSG or Mini Solar System Generator is a small and portable generator that uses solar energy to produce usable electricity for own consumptions. This was made for people who often have no electricity.
